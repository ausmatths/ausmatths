### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a passionate **Software Engineer** specializing in mobile development, enterprise AI/ML systems, and robust full-stack applications. Currently pursuing my MS in Computer Science at Seattle University, I love building high-performance systems that solve complex, real-world problems.

- 🚀 Built **REDvsBLUE**, a live production sports platform with 2M+ lines of code and automated CI/CD pipelines.
- 🧠 Developed **GhostAI v2.0**, an enterprise multi-agent assistant platform achieving 99.9% uptime.
- 🏎️ Engineered an autonomous vehicle trajectory AI that achieves 0.64ms inference time (16x faster than industry standards).
- 👥 Experienced leader, having managed a high-performing team of 26 professionals at Amazon.
