### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a software engineer and AI/ML systems developer focused on building high-performance, self-hosted applications and intelligent automation systems. I recently completed my MS in Computer Science at Seattle University, where I also served as a Graduate Assistant managing technical web infrastructure and core educational deployments.

Before my master's program, I spent three years leading an engineering team of 26 professionals at Amazon. During my tenure, we focused heavily on systems optimization, boosting overall reliability from 94% to 99.7% and streamlining our deployment pipelines to cut release cycles by 35%. I am incredibly passionate about open-source architecture, local-first development, and hardware-level IoT security.

---

### 🚀 Featured Projects

* **REDvsBLUE:** A production-scale sports matchmaking platform engineered from the ground up. Developed with Flutter and Dart for cross-platform delivery, it utilizes Docker and Kubernetes for high-availability orchestration, Sentry for real-time error tracking, and a fully self-hosted MySQL backend to maintain absolute data ownership.
* **GhostAI v2.0:** An enterprise-focused multi-agent orchestration platform designed for secure automation. It relies entirely on local Large Language Models (LLMs) to ensure complete privacy and data security, eliminating external cloud dependencies.
* **Autonomous Trajectory Prediction AI:** Built a custom AI model optimizing autonomous vehicle trajectory paths. By optimizing the inference pipeline, it hits an ultra-low latency of 0.64ms, drastically outperforming generic industry benchmarks.
