### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a software engineer and AI/ML systems developer specializing in full-stack development, AI/ML systems, and cloud-native applications. I recently completed my MS in Computer Science at Seattle University, where I also served as a Graduate Assistant managing technical web infrastructure. 

Before my master's program, I spent three years leading an engineering team of 26 professionals at Amazon. During my tenure, we focused heavily on systems optimization, boosting overall reliability from 94% to 99.7% and reducing mean time to resolution (MTTR) by 35% during peak operations.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

* **REDvsBLUE:** A live cross-platform sports platform engineered with 22 Clean Architecture modules and 99.9% uptime on a Kubernetes-orchestrated backend. Developed using Flutter and Dart, with 682 commits, real-time ELO/Glicko2 matchmaking, and a fully self-hosted MySQL database.
* **GhostAI v2.0:** An enterprise-focused multi-agent orchestration platform. Optimized the inference pipeline to reduce response times by 73% with a 70% memory reduction using intelligent caching, utilizing local LLMs for total privacy.
* **Autonomous Trajectory Prediction AI:** Built a custom multi-sensor fusion architecture achieving an ultra-low latency of 0.64ms (16x faster than industry standards) and a 0.119m prediction accuracy validated across adversarial scenarios.
